About the Challenge

Te Hono Digital Innovation Challenge is a community-focused hackathon where students, software developers, designers, cybersecurity specialists and Māori organisations work together to solve real-world digital challenges.

Over one weekend, multidisciplinary teams collaborate to design practical technology solutions that strengthen communities, preserve knowledge and improve access to digital services.

Our Vision

Bring together people with technical skills and community organisations that have innovative ideas but limited digital resources. Together we create solutions that have lasting value while supporting collaboration, learning and community wellbeing.

Previous Challenge Projects

Every Te Hono event encourages teams to build practical solutions that can continue to grow after the hackathon. The examples below illustrate the types of community-focused projects participants have developed.

Flora Finda

Challenge: Weavers and community members often struggle to find locally available native plants such as harakeke (New Zealand flax) for traditional weaving projects.

Solution: Flora Finda is a community-powered mobile application that allows property owners to register native plants growing on their land. Owners can share the plant species, approximate location, harvesting guidelines and whether the plants are available for community use.

The app promotes sustainable harvesting by encouraging users to respect landowner permissions and local tikanga while connecting people with resources for traditional crafts and environmental projects.

Whether you're an experienced professional or just beginning your technology journey, Te Hono is an opportunity to learn, collaborate and contribute to projects that make a positive impact.
